Bill Payment Statement Manager

Overview: A web-based form that replaces manual spreadsheet entry for tracking delivery and bill payment data across three companies — Rupshi Feed, Rupshi Foods, and City Edible Oil.

Key Features: Fill and save delivery records locally via browser LocalStorage, export data to Google Sheets on demand, company-specific sheet views, and automatic total calculations.

Backend: Google Apps Script acts as a serverless backend, receiving form submissions and writing structured records to the appropriate Google Sheets tab for each company.

Why It Exists: Built to digitize and streamline a manual billing workflow — reducing errors and saving time for day-to-day payment tracking.
